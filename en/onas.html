About us

01

From fabric
to finished apparel.

Danger Wear Production makes custom apparel and club or promotional merchandise in Poland. We work with sports clubs, supporter groups, clothing brands and agencies. We can handle the entire order or a specific stage, such as printing on fabric supplied by the customer.

01

Sportswear

Sports jerseys and apparel for training and competition.

02

Fightwear

Combat sports apparel: rashguards, sets, shorts and leggings.

03

Streetwear

T-shirts, sweatshirts and other customisable clothing.

04

Accessories

Caps, scarves, flags and other items for clubs and brands.

How we work

Our
production

From first contact to finished product — a straightforward process without unnecessary steps.

01

Brief and quotation

We establish the product, fabric, quantity and decoration method, and agree on the scope and schedule.

02

Production preparation

We review print files, sizing and technical details. Production begins once they are approved.

03

Printing and sewing

We produce sublimation prints and sew garments. The scope is tailored to each order, from printing only to a finished product.

04

Quality control and delivery

We inspect the finished garments, assemble the order and prepare it for shipping.
